Maklumat umum sahaja

Kandungan laman ini untuk maklumat umum dan pendidikan sahaja. Ia bukan nasihat pelaburan, cukai atau undang-undang, dan bukan tawaran atau ajakan untuk membeli atau menjual mana-mana produk pelaburan. Keputusan pelaburan perlu dibuat berdasarkan objektif, keadaan kewangan dan profil risiko anda sendiri.

Risiko pelaburan

Pelaburan melibatkan risiko. Pelaburan unit trust dan PRS tertakluk kepada risiko termasuk risiko kehilangan modal. Harga unit dan agihan pendapatan (jika ada) boleh naik dan turun. Prestasi lalu bukan petunjuk prestasi masa depan.

Dokumen produk

Pelabur dinasihatkan membaca dan memahami Prospektus / Memorandum Maklumat (untuk unit trust) atau Disclosure Document (untuk PRS), serta Product Highlights Sheet (PHS), sebelum melabur. Dokumen ini boleh didapati daripada perunding anda atau laman rasmi pengurus dana.

Ilustrasi dan cukai

Contoh dan pengiraan di laman ini hanya ilustrasi, bukan unjuran pulangan. Pengiraan cukai adalah anggaran sahaja; sila rujuk LHDN atau perunding cukai.

Pautan luar

Laman ini memaut ke laman lain, termasuk khairulkhalis.com, laman Manulife Investments dan WhatsApp. Kami tidak bertanggungjawab ke atas kandungan atau amalan privasi laman tersebut.

Servis lain

General information only

The content on this site is for general information and education only. It is not investment, tax or legal advice, and is not an offer or solicitation to buy or sell any investment product. Investment decisions should be based on your own objectives, financial situation and risk profile.

Investment risk

Investing involves risk. Unit trust and PRS investments are subject to risks, including the risk of capital loss. Unit prices and income distributions (if any) may go down as well as up. Past performance is not indicative of future performance.

Product documents

Investors are advised to read and understand the Prospectus / Information Memorandum (for unit trusts) or Disclosure Document (for PRS), and the Product Highlights Sheet (PHS), before investing. These are available from your consultant or the fund manager’s official website.

Illustrations and tax

Examples and calculations on this site are illustrations only, not projections of returns. Tax calculations are estimates only; please refer to LHDN or a tax adviser.
